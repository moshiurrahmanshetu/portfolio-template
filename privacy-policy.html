1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the Reeni Personal Portfolio digital space. Your privacy is a paramount core component of our architectural commitment. This Privacy Policy details the structured procedures, administrative workflows, and compliance guardrails we enforce regarding any potential information processing when you interact with this ThemeForest agency template or subscribe to our newsletter console.

By engaging with our portfolio, utilizing the inquiry interfaces, or registering for our launch beacons, you consent to the collection and programmatic utilization of data as outlined in this agreement. If you do not agree with these provisions, please refrain from submitting private parameters through our system consoles.

2. Information We Collect

We collect variables through two primary vectors: parameters you supply explicitly and structural metrics recorded by our hosting infrastructure automatically.

Static Template Notice

As a valid, premium static HTML5 template, this file itself does not transmit covert background requests. All collection is handled client-side or explicitly routed through configured endpoints.

A. Explicit Personal Data Provided by You:

  • Contact Information: Full names, secure email addresses, corporate telephone numbers, and individual project variables transmitted via the studio's inquiry forms.
  • Subscription Parameters: Names and email coordinates supplied during the coming-soon launch countdown beacon registrations.

B. Automated Technical & Diagnostic Metadata:

  • Diagnostic Parameters: Client browser specifications, IP addresses, operating system environments, and referring routing coordinates.
  • Local Configuration State: Your preferred visual interface theme (light or dark mode) and countdown values stored locally.

3. How Information Is Used

We strictly process the collected variables for legitimate business operations, responsive system maintenance, and authorized communications:

  • Form Processing: To compile, validate, and respond to your direct design inquiries or service requests.
  • Launch Dispatching: To send beta testing alerts, countdown reminders, or new design systems launched for Reeni on ThemeForest.
  • Performance Optimization: Analyzing server logs to guarantee rapid load times and resolve responsive layout rendering issues.

4. Cookies & Trackers

Reeni respects a minimal cookie blueprint. We do not deploy intrusive cross-site tracking systems or commercial advertising trackers.

Cookie Classifications We Employ:

We prioritize LocalStorage over tracking cookies. The key reeni_theme stores your visual interface mode, and reeni_launch_time stores your custom countdown target, completely local to your device.

You can restrict or delete local storage parameters and cookies directly through your browser's security panel. Doing so will reset your custom user configurations on our next preloader boot.

5. Third-Party Services & CDNs

For visual polish and high performance, Reeni loads essential assets via secure, globally distributed CDN networks. These external nodes may log diagnostic IP requests during file transmissions:

  • Google Fonts API: Immersive typography rendering.
  • Bootstrap CDN: Performance stylesheets and grid configurations.
  • AOS Library CDN: Scroll-triggered anim layers.

6. Data Security Measures

We enforce strict security protocols to shield your personal data. All communication channels are protected via active Secure Socket Layer (SSL) or Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption.

However, please be advised that no transmission of data over the global internet or electronic storage infrastructure is guaranteed to be completely secure. We continuously optimize our firewalls, but encourage you to use caution when distributing credentials.

7. Your Legal Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your physical operating jurisdiction, you possess the following protected legal rights regarding your private data:

  • The Right of Access: To request a structured copy of any parameters we hold about your profile.
  • The Right of Rectification: To correct incorrect or outdated contact variables.
  • The Right of Erasure: To request the permanent deletion of your email from our beta newsletter launch maps.

8. Children's Privacy

Our digital portfolio, specialized design systems, and commercial ThemeForest offerings are not engineered for or directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly compile or archive parameters from minors. If you believe a minor has supplied their email through our forms, please contact us immediately to purge the record.

9. Policy Amendments

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy at our discretion to align with updated HTML5 guidelines, browser privacy policies, or ThemeForest licensing revisions.

When amendments are compiled, we will revise the Last Updated indicator displayed at the top of this document card. We advise periodic reviews of this container.
